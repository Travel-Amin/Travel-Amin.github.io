FAQ

Quick answers

What do I need for a visa?

Entry requirements vary by nationality and may change, so we explain what is required for your country when you get in touch and help you prepare the documents.

How much does a trip cost?

We do not list fixed prices on the site because the cost depends on the duration, the services and the number of people. We will send you a clear offer on WhatsApp after the consultation.

How does payment work, and in which currency?

We agree on the payment method and currency clearly with you once the program is settled, and before any commitment.

When is the best time to travel?

It depends on the destination and the type of trip. Tell us what you prefer and we will suggest the most suitable time.

Is my medical information confidential?

We respect your privacy. The site does not store any medical information, and we ask you not to send reports or images before speaking with the advisor on WhatsApp.

Do you guarantee treatment results?

No. We provide consultation and coordination only; medical decisions and results are the responsibility of the treating doctor and center.
